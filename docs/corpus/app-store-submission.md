Title: Submitting an app to the App Store
Source: Apple Developer Documentation, App Store Connect
To submit an app, archive a release build in Xcode, upload it to App Store Connect, and fill in the version metadata: description, screenshots, keywords, privacy information, and pricing. TestFlight lets testers install a pre-release build before review. App Review then checks the build against the review guidelines; a rejection names the guideline and the step to fix. Fix the issue, upload a new build, and submit again.
